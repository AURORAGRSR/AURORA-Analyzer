# AURORA-Analyzer V1.6.31.0 Release Notes

***

## 1. Welcome to V1.6.31.0

Thank you for choosing AURORA-Analyzer! V1.6.31.0 is a dual-theme release. On the visual side, we rebuilt two core visual systems — the aurora starfield and the glass material — **at the morphology level** (not parameter tweaks, but a change of model): the aurora evolves from a "horizontal thin band" into a **hanging curtain** modeled after real auroral physics, and the glass evolves from a dark-blue tinted panel into a **frosty white veil**, refreshing the visual language of the entire application. On the security side, we completed a full audit and systematic hardening of the launch trust chain — integrity verification, token mechanisms, and log privacy have all been upgraded. All improvements take effect automatically, with zero configuration required on your part.

This update focuses on two directions: **visual system renewal** and **security & stability enhancements**.

***

## 2. The All-New Aurora Starfield

### From "Horizontal Band" to a Real Hanging Curtain

If you ever paid close attention to the old aurora, you may have noticed it was essentially a row of ellipses strung along a horizontal path — no matter how it was tuned, it always read as a thin horizontal band, lacking the vertical, ray-like quality of a real aurora. V1.6.31.0 completely rebuilds the aurora morphology based on real auroral physics: each curtain now consists of **dozens of vertical rays plus a glowing base**, brightest at the bottom and fading upward — just like high-altitude gas cascading along magnetic field lines.

### Distinct Top and Bottom Colors, Like Real Atmospheric Emission

A real aurora is reddish-purple at the top and teal-green at the bottom — oxygen atoms at different altitudes emit different colors. The new aurora adds a **vertical color gradient** to every ray: fading from purple-red at the top to teal-green at the bottom, replacing the old flat, single-tone look.

### Rays Flutter Independently, Layered Depth

Each ray has its own oscillation phase, so curtains no longer drift as a rigid block — they ripple with organic, staggered motion. Four curtain layers, each with its own height, hue, and speed, stack into genuine depth. Flow direction reverses with the breathing cycle, layered with diagonal streaks — the aurora now has the mysterious quality of slow, deep breathing.

### Depth Parallax and Star Trails

When switching views, stars move at different speeds across six depth levels — near stars fast, far stars slow — creating a true sense of traveling through space. During parallax motion, near stars stretch into light streaks along the direction of travel, like star trails in a tracking camera shot. Even at rest, the starfield retains a barely perceptible depth breathing, so the scene always feels alive.

### Smoothness Guaranteed

Animation speed is decoupled from your display's refresh rate (no "fast-forwarding" on high-refresh screens), and newly spawned stars appear instantly in a static state with no performance spikes. Performance tiers adapt automatically: the Eco tier trims starfield animation and meteors, while the Performance tier enables everything — smooth on any machine.

## 3. The All-New Frosty Glass

### Uniform, Creamy Frosted Texture

The old glass produced a "sharp center + concentric ghost rings" artifact at large blur radii. The blur kernel has been rewritten to **rebuild sampling weights from the target blur strength** (adaptively switching between 9-tap for small radii and 13-tap for large ones), turning the texture from "sharp core + halos" into uniform cream.

### From Deep-Space Blue to an Icy White Veil

The glass color language switches wholesale to a **frosty aesthetic**: blur radii step up across three tiers to 12/16/20, combined with saturation boost, icy-white tint, and brightness lift — the glass now reads as "a veil brighter than the background," instead of the old darkened blue panel.

### Lit, Three-Dimensional Edges

Glass edges gain an **inner-highlight trio**: a thin white outline all around, a soft gradient at the top edge, and a dark line at the bottom — giving the glass a lit, three-dimensional reading instead of a flat tinted film.

### App-Wide Consistency + Starfield Light Fog

The console box, task HUD, privilege indicator, progress bar, and buttons — every control's glass now speaks the same frosty language for a unified feel across the whole application. Meanwhile, the starfield adds two slowly breathing, drifting deep-space light fogs (teal and icy blue-violet) that give the glass "something to refract" — deep space is no longer 95% near-black, and the glass finally has content behind it.

## 4. Security & Stability Enhancements

This release completes a full security audit and systematic hardening of the launch trust chain. Following the principle of responsible disclosure, this section only outlines the directions of improvement without implementation details:

- **Enhanced integrity verification**: a build-time integrity component now performs both startup-time and runtime verification of all core files; the program refuses to run if verification fails;
- **Upgraded token mechanisms**: launch and elevation tokens gain significantly improved anti-replay and anti-tampering capabilities;
- **Log privacy improvements**: logs no longer output environment-sensitive information such as installation paths;
- **Hardened build pipeline**: multiple verification gates were added to the build pipeline — any failure aborts the build, preventing inconsistent artifacts from shipping.

All of the above take effect automatically with the new version and are fully transparent to your operations and analysis features.

***

## 5. How to Upgrade

This update includes newly added security component files. Please **replace the old version directory entirely**:

1. Back up your user preferences file `Wpf\user-preferences.txt` (if you wish to keep your personalized settings);
2. Extract the new release package and replace the old version directory entirely;
3. Put the backed-up `user-preferences.txt` back into the `Wpf\` directory;
4. Double-click `AURORA-Analyzer.exe` to launch.

**Note**: Do not mix files with the old version or perform partial overwrites, and do not delete any file from the release package — mixing or missing files may cause the integrity check to block the program from running.

The visual system adapts automatically to performance tiers (Eco / Balanced / Performance / Extreme) with zero configuration; lower-end machines downgrade automatically.
