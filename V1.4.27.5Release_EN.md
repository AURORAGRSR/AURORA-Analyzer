# AURORA-Analyzer V1.4.27.5 Release Notes

***

## 1. Welcome to V1.4.27.5

Thank you for choosing AURORA-Analyzer! V1.4.27.5 is a comprehensive upgrade release for the animation and material systems. We have ported the starfield scale enter/exit animation — previously exclusive to the Splash Screen — across every window; systematically optimized the V5 glass material pipeline; and introduced jelly elastic animations for buttons and controls, making every interaction feel alive.

***

## 2. Unified Window Animation Language

### Emerging from the Cosmos

Previously, each window's enter and exit animation parameters varied — some windows scaled up on enter, some scaled down, some shrank on exit, and some merely faded out. V1.4.27.5 unifies all windows to the same animation:

- **Enter**: The window gracefully converges from 1.15x scale to 1.0x, with a fade-in, like a star emerging from afar
- **Exit**: The window scales up from 1.0x to 1.15x, with a fade-out, like a star dissolving into the distance

The core visual metaphor of this animation is "emerging from the cosmos, dissolving into the cosmos," perfectly echoing the app's starfield background theme.

### A More Lingering Exit Ceremony

Exit animation is extended from 450ms to 800ms with softer easing curves. Windows no longer disappear abruptly when closed — instead, they gently scale up and fade away like starlight, bringing a sense of ceremony to every farewell.

***

## 3. More Translucent Liquid Glass Material

V1.4.27.5 systematically optimized the V5 glass material pipeline, making every piece of frosted glass more translucent and refined:

### Blur and Saturation Calibration

Fixed hardcoded blur radius and missing resolution compensation in blur backends. Calibrated AuroraFluentGlass style's BlurRadius from 12 to 8 (blur doubling after backend fix was too strong), while increasing saturation from 1.0 to 1.4 (Mica vibrance +40%), making colors behind the glass more vivid and translucent.

### 14 Optical Layers Refined

Glass material is composed of 14 optical effect layers. This update fine-tuned every single layer: base layer changed from pure cold blue to environment color mixing, specular highlight added secondary light spot and breathing drift, Fresnel layer rewritten from single radial gradient to four-direction linear gradients, bevel layer upgraded from 2-segment to 4-segment gradients, edge highlight reduced overly strong peaks to avoid white edges. Added caustics light spot layer and iridescent interference layer for richer optical quality on the glass surface.

### Rendering Performance Optimization

Changed blur rendering from synchronous to async two-stage pipeline, reducing UI thread CPU overhead from 4ms/frame to 1.5ms/frame. Removed redundant throttling logic, eliminating ~66ms latency for tighter blur tracking during window movement.

***

## 4. Jelly Elastic Interaction Animations

V1.4.27.5 introduced jelly elastic animations for buttons and controls, making interactions feel alive:

### Button Hover-Exit Jelly Rebound

When the mouse leaves a button, the scale no longer returns linearly to 1.0. Instead, it goes through a three-phase jelly rebound: first drops to 0.96 (simulating elastic deformation after release), then rebounds to 1.02 (glass elastic recovery), and finally gracefully settles to 1.0. The entire process takes ~271ms with amplitude within 5%, subtle and natural.

### iOS-Style Bounce Tap Feedback

On button release, the scale slightly overshoots from 0.98 to 1.02 then converges to 1.0, presenting an iOS-style bounce feel. The amplitude is only 2%, barely noticeable, yet gives clicks a "press and rebound" tactile quality.

### Magnet Snap Improvement

The old magnet snap used a damped curve with 3.5% overshoot, where offset would cross center to the opposite side. The new version uses pure EaseOutCubic with no overshoot and no reverse offset. Magnet snap feels like "a light object naturally settling," better matching the soft quality of frosted glass.

### Control Staggered Enter Overshoot Convergence

During control staggered enter, each control first overshoots to 1.07x, then gracefully converges to 1.0 with 525ms QuarticEase, presenting the elastic quality of liquid glass. Window enter also appends a 1.0→1.03→1.0 micro-rebound, and exit wind-ups to 0.97 before scaling away.

***

## 5. Dialog Window Animation Completion

### Elevation Selection Window

Previously, this window's scale transform was incorrectly placed on an inner container, causing the enter animation to fail to recover the scale — controls appeared permanently 10% larger. V1.4.27.5 fixes this issue and aligns the animation to the Splash style.

### Session Restore Window

Previously, this window had no window-level animation at all — it appeared and disappeared instantly. Now it has the complete animation of emerging from and dissolving into the starfield.

### Performance Upgrade Dialog

Previously, this window used a scale-down enter (0.92→1.0) and a simple fade-out exit. Now unified to scale-down convergence enter and scale-up departure exit, consistent with all other windows.

***

## 6. Bug Fixes

### Elevation Window Control Size Anomaly

Fixed an issue where controls in the elevation selection window appeared abnormally enlarged. The root cause was the scale transform being placed at the wrong container level, causing the enter animation to miss its target and the scale to never be recovered.

### Starfield Timer Leak

Fixed an issue where the starfield animation timer continued running in the background after some dialog windows closed, eliminating memory leaks.
