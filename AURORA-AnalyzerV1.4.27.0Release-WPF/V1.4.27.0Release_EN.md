## 1. Welcome to V1.4.27.0

Thank you for choosing AURORA-Analyzer! V1.4.27.0 is a significant upgrade. We have completely revamped the program's visual appearance and interactive experience, bringing you a fresh new look, smooth and fluid animations, and a more stable and reliable user experience. Whether you are a first-time user or upgrading from a previous version, you will notice the thoughtful attention to detail in every corner of this tool.

## 2. New Visual Experience

### Frosted Glass Effect

All windows and panels now feature a premium frosted glass material that looks just like real etched glass. The background is faintly visible through the semi-transparent glass panels, creating a rich sense of depth. The glass surface has a subtle, grainy matte texture that makes the interface feel more authentic and natural, no longer flat or rigid.

### Dynamic Starry Sky Background

The new starry sky background is smoother than ever, completely eliminating the stuttering found in the previous version. The aurora colors are richer and shift and flow as you move your mouse, as if you are controlling a real aurora. Shooting stars and deep-space particles are scattered throughout, bringing the background to life.

### Button Animations

Buttons now have rich and lively animation effects:

| Animation Effect | Description |
|------------------|-------------|
| Magnetic Enlargement | When you hover over a button, it gently enlarges and shifts slightly toward your cursor, as if the button is drawn to it |
| Light Sweep | A shimmering gloss glides slowly across the button surface from left to right, like light reflecting off real glass |
| Elastic Press | The button sinks slightly when clicked, and bounces back with a springy rebound when released, giving a satisfying tactile feel |
| Color Adaptation | The button's color subtly shifts in harmony with the background aurora colors, blending seamlessly into the overall interface |

### Window Animations

The startup screen now features a spring-like elastic entrance animation, adding a touch of ceremony to launching the program. When you switch between different screens, content gracefully exits and then enters in sequence, with clear layering and natural transitions. All animations are extremely smooth, free from the occasional jumps or flickering that could occur in the previous version.

## 3. Performance Improvements

### Smoother Operation

The program now automatically detects your computer's performance level and intelligently selects the optimal effects tier:

| Performance Tier | Best For | Experience |
|------------------|----------|------------|
| Eco Mode | Low-end or older computers | Prioritizes smooth performance, reducing effects appropriately |
| Standard Mode | Mainstream computers | Balances effects and performance for a great experience |
| Enhanced Mode | High-performance computers | Enables all effects for the best visual experience |

You can manually choose to raise or lower the performance tier at startup, giving you flexibility to adapt to your usage scenario.

### Faster Startup

The engine initialization speed has been greatly improved. The new engine runs directly inside the program, eliminating the need for extra communication steps. The startup process is now cleaner and faster, reducing your waiting time.

---

## 4. Feature Improvements

### PRO Mode Console

Log output in PRO mode is now much smoother. Even when a large volume of logs is being generated at the same time, there is no more stuttering. New log entries appear one by one with a graceful slide-in animation, and the text is sharper and clearer, making extended reading less tiring.

### Task Progress Display

A new horizontal step indicator lets you see the progress of the current task at a glance:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Waiting | This step has not started yet |
| Running | This step is currently executing |
| Completed | This step finished successfully |
| Failed | This step encountered an error |

Transitions between statuses include smooth animations, making the overall visual effect clear and intuitive.

### Language Switching

Switching between Chinese and English is now much smoother. When you change the language, each element on the interface transforms in sequence, with the entire process happening seamlessly, eliminating the flickering issue from the previous version.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why do the animations feel faster than before?**

A: The new animations have been specially optimized and adaptively adjusted for different computer configurations, ensuring smooth performance under any conditions without lag or dropped frames.

**Q: Can low-end computers run this program?**

A: Absolutely. The program automatically detects your computer's performance level at startup and switches to Eco Mode to prioritize smooth operation. You can also manually adjust the performance tier.

**Q: Will my settings from the previous version still work?**

A: Yes. All command-line parameters and configuration files are fully compatible. When you upgrade from the previous version, you do not need to make any additional changes — your previous settings will take effect automatically.

**Q: How do I switch performance tiers?**

A: When the program starts, you can select the performance tier from the startup screen. If you wish to adjust it later, you can also change it at any time through the settings menu.
