# AURORA Analyzer Beginner's Guide

> This is a user guide written for everyday people. Don't worry about technical jargon — we'll walk through everything in plain English, one step at a time.

***

## What Is This?

AURORA Analyzer is a tool that **helps you figure out what's wrong with your computer**.

Have you ever been in this situation? Your computer suddenly shows a blue screen, and you have no idea why. Or it's getting slower and slower, but you can't find the cause. Or Windows Update keeps failing and driving you crazy. That's exactly when AURORA Analyzer comes in handy.

What it does is actually quite simple: **like a medical checkup for your computer, it examines everything from top to bottom, tells you what's wrong, and can even help you fix it.**

***

## What Can It Actually Do for Me?

### Export My Computer's Logs

While your Windows computer is running, it quietly records everything that happens — which program crashed, which driver had a problem, when the system reported an error. These records are called "event logs."

But Windows' built-in "Event Viewer" is awful to use — thousands of entries crammed together, and you have no idea where to start.

AURORA Analyzer can automatically **organize these logs into neat tables**, just like an Excel spreadsheet. You can filter by time, by severity, and then export them as a file to send to a tech-savvy friend for help.

***

### Diagnose Problems with My Computer

The most powerful thing about AURORA Analyzer is its built-in "knowledge base" — a database containing the fingerprints of hundreds of common Windows problems.

When you run the diagnostic feature, it:

1. Scans through your computer's logs
2. Compares what it finds against the knowledge base
3. Matches the patterns and tells you: **what the problem is, how serious it is, what might be causing it, and how to fix it**

It checks five main areas:

- **System Stability**: Any blue screens? Unexpected shutdowns? System crashes?
- **Performance**: Is it booting too slowly? Is it laggy? Is the hard drive constantly maxed out?
- **Network**: Is WiFi dropping frequently? Any DNS issues? Can you get online properly?
- **Security**: Is anyone trying to brute-force your password? Any suspicious login activity?
- **Hardware**: Is your hard drive failing? Any memory issues? Is your CPU overheating?

***

### Fix Problems with My Computer

Diagnosing the problem is only half the battle. AURORA Analyzer can also **automatically fix** many common issues.

For example:

- Windows Update keeps failing? It can reset the update components for you
- Can't connect to the network? It can reset your entire network settings
- System files corrupted? It can scan and repair them
- Windows Defender acting up? It can fix your security software

And here's the best part: before every fix, it **automatically creates a backup**. If the fix somehow makes things worse, you can undo it with one click and go right back to where you were. Think of it like saving your game before a boss fight — if you lose, just reload.

***

### Keep Track of My Progress

Sometimes fixing a complex problem takes a long time, and you might need to shut down your computer midway.

AURORA Analyzer automatically saves your progress. When you open it again, it will ask if you want to continue from where you left off. No need to start over from scratch.

## How Do I Get Started?

### Step 1: Open the Program

Find the `AURORA-Analyzer.exe` file and double-click it. **Important: right-click and choose "Run as Administrator"** — many repair operations need administrator privileges.

### Step 2: Choose Your Language

When the program opens, you'll see a dark-themed screen with two buttons — "English" and "简体中文". Click the language you're comfortable with.

### Step 3: Choose Your Mode

Next, you'll see three options:

- **Smart Diagnostic & Auto-Repair Mode**: Choose this if you just want a quick checkup. It will automatically scan, diagnose, and give you recommendations. You don't need to do anything — just follow the prompts.
- **Professional GUI Mode**: Choose this if you want full control over every feature. It opens a complete interface where you can freely choose which logs to export, which areas to diagnose, and which problems to fix.
- **Console Mode**: This is for advanced users who prefer working in the command line. If you're a beginner, you can ignore this option.

**If this is your first time, we recommend choosing "Smart Diagnostic & Auto-Repair Mode." It's the simplest and most worry-free option.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the repair feature safe? Could it mess up my computer?

Before every repair, AURORA Analyzer automatically does two things:

1. Creates a Windows System Restore Point — if anything goes wrong, you can use this to restore your computer to exactly how it was before the repair
2. Backs up the files and registry entries it's about to modify — you can undo any repair at any time

So yes, repairs are safe, and you can always change your mind.

### Does this tool connect to the internet? Will it leak my privacy?

No. Everything AURORA Analyzer does happens right on your computer. It doesn't need an internet connection, and it never uploads any data anywhere. Your logs, your diagnostic reports, and your repair records all stay on your own computer.

### My computer is old. Will it run okay?

AURORA Analyzer automatically detects your computer's specs and chooses the right operating mode. If your computer is on the lower end, it will automatically reduce its resource usage so it doesn't slow you down.

Minimum requirements: Windows 10, 4GB RAM, dual-core processor. Most computers made after 2015 will meet these requirements.
